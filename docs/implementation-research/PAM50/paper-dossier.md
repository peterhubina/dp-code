- **Modalities:** H&E WSI histopathology + transcriptomics (RNA-seq) + MAMMOGRAPHY sequences + clinical covariates, drawn from three non-overlapping cohorts under a modality-disjoint regime in which 'no patient possesses all modalities simultaneously'.
- **Datasets:** NR — the abstract names 'three non-overlapping cohorts' but gives no cohort names and no sample sizes. Full text is paywalled (IEEE Xplore returns HTTP 418/403 to automated fetches; OpenAlex reports any_repository_has_fulltext = false). The first-pass claim of 'TCGA-BRCA n=924' is NOT supported by anything I could verify and appears to have been carried over from the same group's Medical Engineering & Physics paper (row 1).
- **Endpoint:** PAM50 subtype classification ('converts static breast cancer PAM50 subtype classification into a latent trajectory problem'). NUMBER OF CLASSES IS NOT REPORTED in the abstract; whether Normal-like is included is NR; slide-level vs case-level evaluation is NR. Metrics are reported as Balanced Accuracy and MCC, implying a multi-class imbalanced setting, but the label space is not stated.
- **Pipeline:** NR from the abstract — no magnification, patch size, encoder or aggregation is stated. (The first-pass note's 'CTransPath + RNA MLP co-attention' description is NOT in this paper's abstract and appears to be an incorrect carry-over from the same group's Med Eng Phys paper; do not attribute it here without the full text.)

Shared-latent ALIGNMENT across modality-disjoint cohorts: all modalities are mapped into a common 7-dimensional latent space (d_twin = 7, guided by an intrinsic-dimensionality estimate d_id = 6.3 ± 0.4). A PPO-governed stochastic policy then refines the latent state z in R^7 over T = 5 optimisation steps via a Twin-GRU transition model, with a closed-loop latent feedback step after each transition that pulls simulated states toward real patient embeddings by kNN retrieval: z_{t+1} = T(z_t, a_t) + lambda * (1/k) * sum_{i in N(z_t)} (z_i - z_t). Classification is on the refined latent trajectory.
- **Metrics (verbatim):** ABSTRACT ONLY (reconstructed verbatim from the OpenAlex inverted index): 'Multi-seed evaluation (n = 4, training stability only) yields Balanced Accuracy 0.870 ± 0.044 and MCC 0.904 ± 0.046; five-fold cross-validated results confirm stability (Accuracy 0.871 ± 0.029, MCC 0.897 ± 0.038, df = 19). Also: 'PPO is selected for its trajectory geometry properties (broader latent coverage, higher path diversity) rather than marginal accuracy gains, which fall within multi-seed variance.' No AUROC, no per-class metrics, no per-cohort breakdown available. The first-pass '94.93% accuracy' figure is WRONG for this paper — that number belongs to the group's Medical Engineering & Physics paper (row 1).
- **External validation:** 'Cross-cohort' and 'modality-disjoint' by construction across three non-overlapping cohorts, but the abstract does not identify which cohort (if any) is held out as an independent test set, and the reported numbers are seed-variance and 5-fold CV summaries — no clearly external, never-trained-on cohort result is stated. Treat external validation as NR/unclear.
- **Code:** none found (no repository stated in the abstract; the full text is inaccessible, so a code statement inside the paper cannot be ruled out)
- **Caveats:** (1) CORRECTION TO THE FIRST-PASS NOTE: this is NOT a duplicate of the Hezil Medical Engineering & Physics paper. The real abstract describes a completely different method (PPO reinforcement-learning latent trajectory refinement, Twin-GRU transitions, kNN closed-loop feedback), a different modality set (adds MAMMOGRAPHY, drops per-patient fusion), a different regime (modality-disjoint across three cohorts), and different metrics (Balanced Accuracy 0.870, MCC 0.904 — not 94.93% accuracy on TCGA-BRCA n=924). #### P03 · Selective multimodal deep learning for reliable breast cancer subtype classification from histopathology and genomic data

**Hezil N, Bouridane A, Hamoudi R, Al-maadeed S, Akbari Y, Abdullakutty F** — Medical Engineering & Physics 147(3):035006, IOP Publishing / IPEM (venue, volume, issue, article number and publication date 2026-02-23 verified via Crossref)
· DOI/ID: `10.1088/1873-4030/ae449b` · Link: <https://doi.org/10.1088/1873-4030/ae449b> · Evidence: **full-text** · Tier: **core**

- **Modalities:** H&E WSI + bulk RNA-seq (TCGA-BRCA). No clinical variables.
- **Datasets:** TCGA-BRCA, 924 patients with complete PAM50 annotation and matched WSI + RNA-seq. Split 70/15/15 = 647 train / 139 val / 138 test (single stratified random split). No second cohort.
- **Endpoint:** FULL TEXT (publisher page): 4-class PAM50 — Basal, HER2-enriched, Luminal A, Luminal B; Normal-like EXCLUDED. Patient/case-level (one bag of patches + one RNA vector per patient), not slide-level. NOTE CONFLICT: the authors' own released code (github.com/nbldz/..., inspected locally) sets NUM_CLASSES = 5 with CLASS_NAMES = ['Basal','Her2','LumA','LumB','Normal'] — i.e. the shipped code is 5-class incl. Normal-like while the paper text is 4-class.
- **Pipeline:** 20x (0.5 um/pixel), non-overlapping 256x256 px patches, Otsu tissue segmentation + Macenko stain normalisation; frozen 'CTransPath' ViT encoder -> 768-d patch embeddings; attention pooling to a slide/patient-level vector. CAVEAT (verified by reading the released code, main.py:286-290): the encoder actually instantiated is timm 'swin_tiny_patch4_window7_224' with ImageNet weights, i.e. ImageNet Swin-Tiny, NOT the pathology-SSL CTransPath weights the paper claims — which plausibly explains the very weak 62.00% WSI-only result.
- **Fusion:** Pooled-late (embedding-level) fusion of the attention-pooled WSI vector with the RNA-seq MLP embedding in a shared latent space; three operators compared — (a) concatenation, (b) gated fusion (learned gate), (c) cross-attention with RNA features as queries and WSI features as keys/values. On top of these sits a DECISION-LEVEL 'smart routing' step: if the RNA-only head's max softmax >= t*=0.75 the RNA-only prediction is emitted, otherwise the sample is deferred to the multimodal (cross-attention) model.
- **Metrics (verbatim):** Test-set (n=138, single held-out split; NO cross-validation, NO confidence intervals), verbatim from the full text: RNA-Only accuracy 92.11%, macro F1 0.90, weighted F1 0.91, ECE 0.118. WSI-Only accuracy 62.00%, macro F1 0.57, weighted F1 0.64. Concatenation accuracy 85.31% (precision 0.85 / recall 0.85 / macro F1 0.85 / weighted F1 0.85). Gated fusion accuracy 85.12% (0.85 / 0.85 / 0.85 / 0.85). Cross-attention (fusion-only) accuracy 94.23%, ECE 0.094. Routing-based (proposed) accuracy 94.93%, precision 0.9461, recall 0.9438, macro F1 0.9447, weighted F1 0.9492, ECE 0.061. Routing statistics: 38.2% of test samples routed to multimodal inference; 61.8% handled by RNA-Only at 97.6% accuracy; 3.12x computational speed-up. (The 94.93%, 38.2% and 3.12x figures are independently confirmed in the PubMed abstract, which I read directly.)
- **External validation:** none — single TCGA-BRCA cohort, single random split, no independent cohort and no tissue-source-site holdout.
- **Code:** https://github.com/nbldz/Selective-Multimodal-Deep-Learning-for-Breast-Cancer-Subtype-Classification (HTTP 200; a local read-only clone of this repo exists in this project and was inspected for the code-level caveats above)
- **Caveats:** (1) CIRCULAR TARGET: PAM50 is itself a 50-gene transcriptomic signature, so an RNA-seq input head largely re-derives the label; RNA-only alone reaches 92.11% while WSI-only reaches 62.00%, so essentially all of the headline 94.93% rides on the RNA head and the routing mechanism explicitly falls back to RNA-only for 61.8% of cases. The paper does not treat this as leakage. (2) PREPROCESSING LEAKAGE in the released code, verified firsthand: imputation, top-variance gene selection and StandardScaler are all fit on the FULL dataset (main.py:125-146) BEFORE train_test_split (main.py:905-909), so test statistics leak into feature selection/scaling. (3) Single stratified 70/15/15 split with random_state=42; no k-fold CV, no repeated seeds, no confidence intervals, and the test set is only n=138 patients across 4 (or 5) classes — a handful of HER2-enriched cases drive the macro F1. (4) No patient-level *site* holdout; TCGA tissue-source-site signal is a known confounder. (5) Paper says 4 classes / code says 5 — unresolved. (6) 'CTransPath' is ImageNet Swin-Tiny in the released code. (7) Table rows other than the abstract-confirmed numbers were extracted from the IOP article page by an automated page read, so individual per-row values carry mild transcription risk; an earlier note in this project recorded slightly different figures (RNA-only 91%, routing 95.05%), suggesting version drift between a preprint and the published version. (8) Same group as row 7 (CDLS / IEEE JBHI) — treat the two as one research programme, not independent corroboration.

Aggregation = plain arithmetic MEAN of patch feature vectors, z = (1/N) sum x_i, giving one 1xF slide vector (no attention, no MIL). That vector goes through three 1D convolutional layers -> global average pooling -> 138-unit linear output. Adam, lr=0.001, MSE loss, minibatch 12, early stopping patience 4, max 150 epochs, 20-50 min on a single V100 (vs 4 GPUs x 8.48 h for a patch-based baseline).

The architecture is a two-stage CASCADE — image -> predicted gene expression (deep regression) -> subtype (soft-voting ensemble of RF/MLP/LDA/LR at the patient level). (8) Aggregation is naive mean pooling with an ImageNet backbone — a much weaker imaging pipeline than UNI2-h + CLAM attention MIL, which makes its subtype AUROCs a soft baseline rather than a competitive target. #### P49 · Predicting breast cancer types on and beyond molecular level in a multi-modal fashion

**Zhang T; Tan T; Han L; Appelman L; Veltman J; Wessels R; Duvivier KM; Loo C; Gao Y; Wang X; Horlings HM; Beets-Tan RGH; Mann RM** — npj Breast Cancer, vol. 9, art. 16 (22 Mar 2023) [verified via Crossref]
· DOI/ID: `10.1038/s41523-023-00517-2` · Link: <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41523-023-00517-2> · Evidence: **full-text** · Tier: **background**

- **Modalities:** Two RADIOLOGY imaging modalities only: mammography (CC + MLO views) and ultrasound. NO omics, NO gene expression, NO histopathology, NO WSI. 'Multi-modal' here means mammography + ultrasound, not imaging + molecular.
- **Datasets:** Single-centre retrospective cohort, Jan 2010 - Nov 2019: 3,360 paired cases (each with mammography and matching ultrasound). Training cohort 2,688 (80.0%), test cohort 672 (20.0%), observer-study subset 168 (25% of the test cohort, i.e. 5.0% of all). Class counts (all / train / test): Luminal A 1,494 / 1,214 / 280; Luminal B 905 / 721 / 184; HER2-enriched 386 / 298 / 88; Triple-negative 575 / 455 / 120. Within the training cohort, a further random 4:1 train/val split. 112 of the 672 test cases had an equivocal HER2 IHC score of 2+.
- **Endpoint:** NO PAM50 METRIC, AND NO PAM50 CONTENT AT ALL - the string 'PAM50' does not appear anywhere in the full text. The endpoint is a 4-category IHC-SURROGATE molecular subtype: Luminal A / Luminal B / HER2-enriched / Triple-negative, assigned from immunohistochemistry for ER, PR, HER2 and Ki67, with silver-enhanced in situ hybridisation (SISH) used only to resolve HER2 IHC 2+ equivocal cases. No RNA was sequenced and no intrinsic-subtype (PAM50/genefu/Prosigna) call was ever made. A secondary binary endpoint is Luminal vs Non-Luminal, which the authors state is equivalent to ER+ vs ER-. Case-level (radiologist-cropped lesion patches per case, one prediction per case).
- **Pipeline:** n/a (no WSI). Radiology pipeline: dedicated breast radiologists crop lesion patches from mammography (CC and MLO) and ultrasound; all images resized to 256x256 and intensity-normalised; per-modality ResNet50 backbone initialised from ImageNet with all weights trainable (ResNet34/50/101 and Inception-v3 compared; ResNet50 selected); intra-modality self-attention (Q/K/V, channels reduced 8x by 1x1 conv) plus inter-modality self-attention plus inter channel-and-spatial attention (CBAM-style); global average pooling -> fully connected -> softmax. Adam, lr 1e-3 decayed 0.9 every 10 epochs, batch 32, 200 epochs, NVIDIA RTX A6000 48 GB, on-the-fly intensity and geometry augmentation. Grad-CAM and t-SNE for interpretability.
- **Fusion:** Intermediate / feature-level (mid-network) fusion: separate ResNet50 streams for mammography and ultrasound whose feature maps are fused through intra-modality self-attention followed by inter-modality self-attention and inter channel+spatial attention, then pooled by a shared GAP+FC head. NOT decision-level, NOT patch-MIL. Ablation ladder given: Multi-ResNet50 (plain concat) -> MulR-interSA -> MulR-iiSA -> MulR-interCSA -> MDL-IIA.
- **Metrics (verbatim):** All verbatim, all on the single held-out test cohort n=672 (internal held-out split of one centre; no CV, no external set); brackets are 95% CIs. 4-category subtype task, proposed MDL-IIA: Accuracy 88.5% [86.0, 90.9], Precision 87.8% [85.0, 90.7], Recall 85.4% [82.2, 88.4], F1 0.862 [0.831, 0.892], MCC 0.837 [0.803, 0.870]. Unimodal baselines: US-ResNet50 Acc 81.1% / MCC 0.731 [0.689, 0.772]; MG-ResNet50 Acc 82.0% / MCC 0.744 [0.704, 0.785]; naive multimodal Multi-ResNet50 Acc 84.4% / MCC 0.777 [0.736, 0.814] (multimodal > each unimodal, both p<0.001); best competitor Multi-ResNet50+SE Acc 86.0% / MCC 0.801 [0.763, 0.838]. Binary Luminal vs Non-Luminal, MDL-IIA on n=672: AUC 0.929 [0.903, 0.951], accuracy 93.4% [91.5, 95.2], sensitivity 98.5% [97.3, 99.6], specificity 82.2% [76.9, 87.4], PPV 92.5% [90.1, 94.7], NPV 96.1% [92.9, 98.8] (vs Multi-ResNet50 AUC 0.858 [0.821, 0.893] and Multi-ResNet50+SE AUC 0.902 [0.871, 0.929], both p<0.001). Observer study n=168: radiologists' 4-class accuracy 56.5-68.0%, MCC 0.428-0.568; 6-reader majority vote accuracy 72.6%, MCC 0.630; MDL-IIA accuracy 84.4% (p<0.001) and MCC 0.780 (p<0.001); MDL-IIA AUC 0.920 [0.865, 0.967] for Luminal vs Non-Luminal, sensitivity 0.965 [0.929, 0.992] / specificity 0.810 [0.696, 0.913] vs reader panel sensitivity 0.837 [0.760, 0.898] / specificity 0.752 [0.628, 0.870]. On the 112 HER2 IHC 2+ equivocal test cases the model reached 86.6% accuracy (97/112). No macro AUROC is reported for the 4-class task - only MCC/accuracy/precision/recall/F1.
- **External validation:** None. Explicitly listed as a limitation: single-centre feasibility study, one mammography system and one ultrasound system, 'Subsequent studies will collect more cases from multiple centers to externally verify the performance of the model.' All reported numbers are an internal 80/20 held-out split (plus a 168-case subset of that same test set for the reader study).
- **Code:** Yes: https://github.com/Netherlands-Cancer-Institute/Multimodal_attention_DeepLearning (academic use only). Data: private, not publicly available; source-data Excel for main figures/tables provided with the article.
- **Caveats:** (1) THE HEADLINE 'MOLECULAR SUBTYPE' IS NOT PAM50. Labels are the IHC surrogate (ER/PR/HER2/Ki67) - the paper never mentions PAM50, genefu, Prosigna, RNA-seq or any expression-based intrinsic subtype. Under a strict rule of 'no PAM50 content at all -> excluded' this row is excludable; I kept it as 'background' because it is still a 4-class breast molecular-subtype classification with an explicit multimodal fusion ablation, i.e. a useful methodological foil - but its numbers are NOT comparable to a PAM50 result and must never be quoted as such. (2) THE ABSTRACT'S 'gene-level ground truth' IS MISLEADING. It refers solely to SISH (a DNA in-situ hybridisation amplification test) applied to HER2 IHC 2+ equivocal cases - not to gene expression and not to a molecular subtype call. Do not let this phrase upgrade the paper into a gene-expression-labelled study. (3) MCC 0.837 is a correlation coefficient, not an AUROC - it is not directly comparable to macro-AUROC numbers in other rows. No per-class AUROC or macro AUROC is given for the 4-class task. (4) SPLITTING GRANULARITY UNVERIFIED: the paper splits 3,360 'cases' 80/20 and says the training cohort was then 'randomly divided into training set and validation set at a ratio of 4:1'. It never states that the split was made at PATIENT level, so a patient with bilateral or multifocal disease (or repeat exams over the 2010-2019 window) could in principle appear in both train and test. Unverifiable from the text - flag as a leakage risk, not a confirmed defect. (5) Single centre, single MG vendor, single US vendor, retrospective; the authors concede the model 'may need to be retrained for data from other systems'. (6) Lesion patches were cropped by dedicated breast radiologists, i.e. the pipeline presupposes a human-localised lesion; performance is not end-to-end from the full image. (7) HER2-enriched (n=88) and TN (n=120) are the small test classes and the confusion matrix shows they are recognised worse than the luminals; the strong overall accuracy is carried by the 464/672 luminal majority. (8) Reader-study comparison is somewhat unfair by the authors' own admission - radiologists are not trained to assign molecular subtype from imaging.

#### P50 · Predicting Molecular Phenotypes from Histopathology Images: A Transcriptome-Wide Expression-Morphology Analysis in Breast Cancer

**Wang Y, Kartasalo K, Weitz P, Ács B, Valkonen M, Larsson C, Ruusuvuori P, Hartman J, Rantalainen M** — Cancer Research (AACR) 2021;81(19):5115-5126 [volume/issue/pages confirmed from Europe PMC core metadata + JATS full text]
· DOI/ID: `NR` · Link: <https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-21-0482> · Evidence: **full-text** · Tier: **background**

- **Modalities:** H&E WSI only (unimodal). RNA-seq is the prediction TARGET, not an input modality. Independent NanoString GeoMx spatial-transcriptomics and IHC Ki67 data used only as orthogonal validation of predictions.
- **Datasets:** Development (Clinseq-BC N=270 + TCGA-BC N=721 = 991 patients, 1 WSI each, 7.28M tiles) split patient-wise into train N=558 (56.30%, 4.08M tiles), tuning N=139 (14.03%, 0.97M tiles), validation N=122 (12.31%, 0.90M tiles), internal test N=172 (17.36%, 1.33M tiles). External test: ABiM N=350 (GSE81538). Spatial-transcriptomics validation: 24 tumors selected from an independent set of 168; analyses report 22 individuals, 12 ROIs each = 264 ROIs, 76 genes. Ki67 IHC comparison: 37 IHC-HE tumor pairs in the test set.
- **Endpoint:** NOT a PAM50 subtype classifier. Endpoint is per-gene REGRESSION of continuous mRNA expression: one independent InceptionV3 CNN per gene for 17,695 genes (from 20,477 measured; 19,112 non-zero variance; variance>0.01 filter), predicted at tile level and averaged to slide/patient level ('EMO-average'), plus tile-level 'EMO-spatial'. Secondary endpoint is a continuous mRNA proliferation score = mean expression of 11 proliferation genes taken from the PAM50 panel. Labels are RNA-seq derived continuous expression values, NOT PAM50 subtype calls and NOT IHC surrogates. One WSI per patient, so slide-level == patient-level. PAM50 subtype labels appear only as an external reference against which unsupervised clusterings are compared (adjusted Rand index) and to stratify proliferation-score distributions in figures — no 4-class (or any-class) subtype classification model, no subtype AUROC/accuracy/F1 anywhere.
- **Pipeline:** FFPE H&E slides; Clinseq-BC and ABiM scanned in-house on Hamamatsu NanoZoomer XR at x40 (0.226 um/px); TCGA slides scanned at 20x were EXCLUDED. Tiling into 598x598 px tiles (271x271 um); per-tile sharpness QC; stain colour normalisation; tumour detection/segmentation restricting analysis to invasive cancer regions. Backbone InceptionV3 with last layer replaced by a single neuron + linear activation (regression); Adam, lr=1e-6, MSE loss, minibatch 32 tiles, 150 steps/epoch, early stopping (min delta 0.003, patience 80), max 500 epochs; 90-degree rotation/flip augmentation. Aggregation = simple MEAN of tile-level predictions per slide (no attention/MIL). ~300,000 GPU hours total (one model per gene, run in parallel on V100s).
- **Fusion:** unimodal (image-only). No multimodal fusion of any kind; expression is the regression target.

Model-selection for testing: 1,011 genes with R2_pred>0.2 and FDR-adj P<0.001 in validation carried forward. INTERNAL TEST (N=172, held-out): 876/1,011 genes (86.65%) with significant predicted-vs-observed Spearman association (Bonferroni-adj P<0.05); 479 of these with R2_pred>0.2. EXTERNAL TEST (ABiM, N=350): 908/995 genes (91.26%) validated (Bonferroni-adj Spearman P<0.05); R2_pred deliberately not computed externally (scale differences). Gene-panel coverage: Prosigna/PAM50 29 of 50 genes successfully validated; Oncotype DX 10 of 21 genes brought forward and validated; EndoPredict 3 of 12. PAM50-related clustering (NOT classification): consensus clustering into 4 clusters on PAM50 genes vs RNA-seq clustering, adjusted Rand index 0.25 (95% CI 0.23-0.27, permutation P<0.001) internal test and 0.20 (95% CI 0.19-0.22, P<0.001) external test; agreement of clusterings with INTRINSIC SUBTYPE LABELS: adjusted Rand index 0.1 for EMO-average prediction (95% CI 0.08-0.13, P<0.001) vs 0.27 for RNA-seq (95% CI 0.25-0.29, P<0.001); gene-side (PAM50) clustering ARI 0.73 internal / 0.71 external. Proliferation score (11 PAM50 proliferation genes): Spearman rho 0.67 (P=2.82e-17) validation, 0.66 (P=4.32e-23) internal test, 0.55 (P=1.40e-29) external test; slide-level IHC Ki67 vs P.Score(EMO) Spearman 0.61 (P=5.12e-5, n=37 pairs); spatial LME association P<2e-16. Spatial transcriptomics: 59/76 genes (77.63%) with EMO-spatial predictions significantly associated with GeoMx expression (FDR-adj P<0.05, likelihood-ratio test, N_WSI=22). GSEA: 16 significant Reactome pathways and 7 Hallmark pathways (FDR-adj P<0.05).
- **External validation:** Yes for the gene-expression endpoint: ABiM cohort N=350 (independent Swedish cohort, GSE81538, scanned in-house), used once at the end; 908/995 genes validated, proliferation score Spearman 0.55. No external (or internal) validation of any PAM50 subtype classification, because no such classifier exists in the paper.
- **Code:** NR — no code-availability statement found in the Europe PMC JATS full text. Only third-party repos are cited for preprocessing: https://github.com/Peter554/StainTools and https://github.com/schaugf/HEnorm_python. ABiM expression data are public (GSE81538); TCGA WSIs from GDC.
- **Caveats:** (1) MOST IMPORTANT: this paper reports NO PAM50 subtype classification metric. PAM50 enters only three ways — 29/50 PAM50 genes validated as individual expression regressions; unsupervised consensus clustering in PAM50 gene space compared to intrinsic subtype labels by adjusted Rand index (0.1 for EMO predictions vs 0.27 for real RNA-seq, i.e. predicted expression recovers intrinsic subtype structure only weakly and worse than RNA-seq itself); and an 11-gene proliferation signature borrowed from the PAM50 panel. Do not read the ARI 0.25/0.20 numbers as subtype accuracy — they measure cluster-to-cluster agreement between two clusterings, not label prediction. (2) ERBB2 (HER2) could NOT be predicted by the CNN — explicitly stated — which is a direct warning for the HER2-enriched class in any downstream subtyping. (3) Label basis is RNA-seq expression, not IHC surrogate; IHC is used only descriptively (violin plots of predicted expression by ER/PR/HER2 IHC status) with no AUROC reported. (6) Selection effects: TCGA slides scanned at 20x were excluded; for ABiM, the WSI chosen per patient was the clinically-flagged block or the one with largest predicted tumour area. (7) Small-n sub-analyses: 22 tumors / 264 ROIs for spatial validation, 37 IHC-HE pairs for Ki67.

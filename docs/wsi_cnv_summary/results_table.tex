\scriptsize
\setlength{\parskip}{1.5pt}
\textsuperscript{*}\,/\,\textsuperscript{ns}: the 95\% bootstrap interval on the paired
difference excludes\,/\,includes zero. $\Delta$ is the arm minus the equal-weight probability
mean of its own cohort, so a negative value is worse than the untrained baseline.

$^{\dagger}$\,Post hoc: computed on CPTAC after inspection; no CPTAC tuning of any model. Nothing
in this work was refit, calibrated or thresholded on CPTAC.

\textsuperscript{a}\,Protocols. Internal: 10 CLAM folds pooled out-of-fold on the 599 TCGA cases
that reach a test fold, the CNV arm refit per CLAM fold, paired bootstrap $N=2000$ at seed 13.
External: TCGA-trained models applied to CPTAC untouched, paired bootstrap $N=4000$ at seed 7.
The two bootstrap seeds differ deliberately and are not unified.

\textsuperscript{b}\,The equal-weight probability mean is \emph{untrained}: it averages the two
four-class probability vectors element-wise and has no parameters. It is the baseline, not the
WSI-only model.

\textsuperscript{c}\,No trained fusion operator has an external evaluation path: \texttt{dp-evaluate}
refuses \texttt{film\_attention} and \texttt{coattn} checkpoints by name and defaults to the TCGA
test split, so the five operators are internal-only and every external fusion row above is the
untrained probability mean of two independently trained arms.

\textsuperscript{d}\,CLAM's 10 splits are drawn independently rather than partitioned: 599 of 910
cases reach a test fold and 242 of them are tested by two to five models, so the pooled WSI-only
figure is a small ensemble, flattered by roughly $+0.01$ macro AUROC. All six trained internal
runs share the split set, so the internal ladder comparison stays self-consistent.

\textsuperscript{e}\,Per-class recall for the five trained operators is not produced by any script
in the repository and exists nowhere on disk, hence n/a.

\textsuperscript{f}\,The CNV arm \emph{as a model in its own right} scores macro AUROC
$0.866 \pm 0.003$ (5-fold CV averaged over 10 reseeds, 945 TCGA non-Normal cases; the $\pm$ is the
spread over reseeds, not a bootstrap CI), and aneuploidy burden alone (one feature, mean absolute
$\log_2$, 5-fold at seed 0, the published protocol) reaches 0.685 on the same set. The internal
0.872 above is the per-CLAM-fold refit; the same 599 cases under
\texttt{StratifiedKFold(10, seed 0)} give 0.862 with an equal-weight mean of 0.922, so name the
protocol whenever an internal CNV number is quoted.

\title{\vspace{-1.2em}H\&E + arm-level CNV fusion for PAM50 subtype:\\
internal and external evaluation summary}
\author{dp-code project (compiled from on-disk results)}
\date{2026-09-01}

\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Does conditioning H\&E whole-slide images on arm-level copy number beat averaging two independent
predictions for four-class PAM50 subtyping? No. Trained on TCGA-BRCA and validated on CPTAC-BRCA
(114 cases, 378 slides, never trained on), the untrained equal-weight probability mean of a
CLAM-MB / UNI2-h WSI model and a 39-arm copy-number logistic regression reaches macro AUROC
0.909 [0.861, 0.949] externally, against 0.847 for WSI alone and 0.888 for CNV alone; a post hoc
prior-balanced mean reaches 0.912. Internally, on 599 pooled out-of-fold TCGA cases with the CNV
arm refit per CLAM fold, the mean reaches 0.926 against WSI 0.887 and CNV 0.872, and all five
jointly trained fusion operators score 0.882 to 0.899, every one significantly below that
untrained mean. CNV alone is reported with every fusion number: externally the equal-weight mean
is not distinguishable from it.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setup}

\textbf{Task and unit.} Four-class PAM50 subtype (Luminal A, Luminal B, Basal-like,
HER2-enriched); Normal-like is dropped and CPTAC contains no Normal-like case, so the cohorts
share a label space. The prediction unit is the case: slide-level probabilities are mean-pooled to
the case before any metric is computed. Macro AUROC is one-vs-rest.

\textbf{Arms.} The WSI arm is UNI2-h 1536-dimensional patch features aggregated by CLAM-MB
(\texttt{model\_size big}, dropout 0.5, inverse-frequency weighted sampler, so no claim of
``natural class frequencies'' applies), 10 folds at seed 1 on the tracked split set
\texttt{splits/tcga\_brca\_subtyping\_100}. The CNV arm is 39 chromosome arms, each feature the
median gene-level $\log_2$ ratio over that arm, which is the scale deployed low-pass assays
report; its classifier is a frozen constant defined once in \texttt{tools/pam50\_arms.py},
\texttt{StandardScaler} $\rightarrow$
\texttt{LogisticRegression(max\_iter=4000, C=0.1, class\_weight='balanced')}, refit in process and
never stored as a checkpoint. Copy number rather than RNA is the second modality because the PAM50
labels are computed from the expression matrix itself, so an RNA branch leaks the target by
construction; the project's WSI+RNA number is inflated for that reason and is not quoted here.

\textbf{Fusion.} The untrained \emph{equal-weight probability mean} averages the two four-class
probability vectors element-wise; it has no parameters, was fixed on TCGA before CPTAC was scored,
and is the baseline every trained operator must clear. Five trained operators (\texttt{concat},
\texttt{gated}, \texttt{cross\_attention}, \texttt{film\_attention}, \texttt{coattn}) pair the
CLAM-MB branch with a two-layer tabular encoder over the 39 arms
(\texttt{tabular\_hidden\_dim 64}, \texttt{fusion\_hidden\_dim 32}), all warm-started from the
corresponding fold's WSI-only checkpoint and not frozen; \texttt{film\_attention} conditions the
attention network's input through a rank-16 FiLM bottleneck with 0.2 modality dropout, and
\texttt{coattn} runs co-attention between patch tokens and 22 chromosome tokens. A sixth operator,
\texttt{residual}, refuses at composition time because no supported trainer produces its tabular
checkpoint, so the ladder is five operators, not six.

\textbf{Protocols and frozen constants.} Internal metrics are pooled out-of-fold: held-out
predictions concatenated over the ten folds and scored once, a different quantity from the mean of
ten per-fold AUCs in CLAM's \texttt{summary.csv} (0.893 there against 0.887 pooled; never mix
them). Internal contrasts use a paired bootstrap of $N=2000$ at seed 13 with the CNV arm refit per
CLAM fold; external contrasts use $N=4000$ at seed 7; a third bootstrap, $N=2000$ at seed 11,
belongs to the stacking analysis and is not quoted here. The three seeds differ deliberately and
unifying them would move published intervals. Resamples that lose a class are rejected so macro
AUROC stays defined. Two class orders exist, CLAM's \texttt{LumA, LumB, Basal, Her2} and
\texttt{pam50\_arms.CLASSES} sorted \texttt{Basal, Her2, LumA, LumB}; they are bridged by
\texttt{clam\_column\_order()} before anything is scored across them, and every table here is in
the sorted order.

\section{Results}

\input{results_table.tex}

\textbf{Internal (TCGA-BRCA, 599 pooled out-of-fold cases).} The untrained equal-weight mean
reaches macro AUROC 0.926 [0.910, 0.941] and balanced accuracy 0.751, above WSI alone (0.887) and
CNV alone (0.872, refit per CLAM fold). All five trained operators land between 0.882
(\texttt{film\_attention}) and 0.899 (\texttt{coattn}), and each is significantly below the mean
on a paired bootstrap, by $-$0.027 to $-$0.044 macro AUROC; no trained operator reaches even the
better unimodal arm plus the gap that averaging buys. HER2 recall moves with AUROC: 26/51 for WSI,
25/51 for CNV, 30/51 for the mean. The mechanism is error correlation, quantified in
Section~\ref{sec:contrasts}.

\textbf{External (CPTAC-BRCA, 114 cases, never trained on).} The ordering is preserved and the
HER2 story changes completely. WSI alone reaches 0.847 with HER2 recall 0/14, not one of the
fourteen HER2-enriched cases recovered. CNV alone reaches 0.888 with 12/14, and the equal-weight
mean 0.909 with 6/14, the mean inheriting the WSI arm's confident errors on that class. The post
hoc prior-balanced mean reaches 0.912 with 10/14 and the best balanced accuracy (0.740), but it is
post hoc and its AUROC gain over the equal-weight mean is not significant. CNV alone remains the
arm to beat: the equal-weight mean does not separate from it, and only the post hoc
prior-balanced mean does, with a lower bound that prints as $+0.000$. No trained operator appears
in the external block because none can be evaluated externally.

Basal-like is near-saturated for every arm (0.967 to 0.992) and is the class most exposed to the
aneuploidy-burden control: one genome-instability feature already reaches 0.685 macro AUROC on the
945-case fitting set (5-fold at seed 0, the published protocol), so Basal $\approx 0.97$ must be
read beside that number rather than as evidence that the arm pattern does all the work. LumB is
the weakest class everywhere and, at $n=17$, the least certain.

\section{Key contrasts}
\label{sec:contrasts}

Deltas are paired bootstraps on the same case set; \emph{sig} means the 95\% interval excludes
zero. Internal: $N=2000$, seed 13, CNV refit per CLAM fold. External: $N=4000$, seed 7.
Source: \texttt{data/contrasts.csv}.

\begin{itemize}
\setlength{\itemsep}{1pt}
\item \textbf{Internal, mean vs.\ WSI alone:} AUROC $+0.039$ [$+0.023$, $+0.054$] sig; balanced
accuracy $+0.074$ [$+0.027$, $+0.121$] sig.
\item \textbf{Internal, mean vs.\ CNV alone:} AUROC $+0.054$ [$+0.037$, $+0.073$] sig; balanced
accuracy $+0.073$ [$+0.033$, $+0.116$] sig.
\item \textbf{Internal, each trained operator vs.\ the mean:} negative and sig throughout, from
$-0.027$ [$-0.043$, $-0.011$] (\texttt{coattn}, best) to $-0.044$ [$-0.062$, $-0.027$]
(\texttt{film\_attention}, worst); balanced accuracy sig-negative for all five.
\item \textbf{External, equal-weight mean vs.\ WSI alone:} $+0.063$ [$+0.024$, $+0.104$] sig.
\item \textbf{External, equal-weight mean vs.\ CNV alone:} $+0.021$ [$-0.003$, $+0.047$],
\emph{ns}. Averaging does not measurably improve on copy number alone externally.
\item \textbf{External, post hoc prior-balanced mean vs.\ CNV alone:} $+0.024$ [$+0.000$,
$+0.050$] sig, the lower bound exactly $+0.000$ at three decimals (full precision $+0.00047$).
This is the whole edge of fusion over CNV alone, and it is post hoc.
\item \textbf{External, post hoc prior-balanced mean vs.\ equal-weight mean:} AUROC $+0.003$
[$-0.005$, $+0.011$] \emph{ns}; balanced accuracy $+0.093$ [$+0.014$, $+0.177$] sig.
\item \textbf{External, CNV alone vs.\ WSI alone:} AUROC $+0.042$ [$-0.019$, $+0.103$]
\emph{ns}; balanced accuracy $+0.202$ [$+0.089$, $+0.315$] sig.
\item \textbf{Error correlation $\phi$ between per-case errors.} Among the five trained operators,
mean over the ten operator pairs, internal pooled out-of-fold: $\phi = 0.656$ (0.582 to 0.706).
Between the two unimodal arms, internal, CNV refit per CLAM fold: $\phi = 0.193$; the same pair
under \texttt{StratifiedKFold(10, seed 0)}, which is the published value: $\phi = 0.269$.
Externally, raw WSI vs.\ CNV: $\phi = -0.006$, either arm right on 91.2\% of cases while each
alone is right on 70.2\%. Joint training collapses the diversity that averaging exploits.
\end{itemize}

For context, and as the only comparable published work pairing copy number as the sole molecular
modality with H\&E for PAM50, Amer 2025 reports CNV+Image 0.8835 against CNV alone 0.8284, and its
own untrained ``Simple Ensemble'' at 0.9074 beats its trained operators. The pattern here is the
same one, extended to an external cohort and reported alongside the CNV-alone arm.

\section{Caveats and limitations}

\begin{itemize}
\setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}

\item \textbf{The external HER2 collapse is domain shift, not calibration.} 0/14 survives a
12-fold prior boost and SLD-EM label shift (that arm, post hoc, gives 0.858 with recall still 0/14
and an implied HER2 prior of 0.000). The mechanism is UNI2-h feature-space geometry: cohort of
origin is readable from a single patch feature at AUROC 1.0000, the cohort shift is 5.4 times the
HER2 class signal, and a logistic probe on slide-mean features reproduces 0/378, which exonerates
the MIL head. Aggregation, segmentation dilution, label mismatch and a 17q-specific explanation
were refuted; CNV's 12/14 holds without 17q.

\item \textbf{Power.} External HER2 is $n=14$ and LumB $n=17$; per-class external numbers for
those classes, and any recall expressed as k/14, are indicative only.

\item \textbf{The ladder is a near-baseline, not a matched, comparison.} The WSI baseline uses
sweep-selected optimiser values with \texttt{inst\_loss svm} and instance clustering on; the five
operators use rounded learning rate and weight decay, CLAM's default bag weight 0.7 and
\texttt{-{}-no\_inst\_cluster}. Reproduced as run rather than tidied.

\item \textbf{Shared warm start, unseparated confound.} All five operators warm-start from the
same \texttt{pam50\_final\_s1} checkpoints, so joint-training collapse and shared-initialisation
diversity collapse cannot be told apart from these runs; a \texttt{-{}-no\_warm\_start} arm would
settle it and has not been run.

\item \textbf{CPTAC and TCGA features are geometrically comparable but not identically
preprocessed} (CLAM tissue segmentation for TCGA, Trident for CPTAC).

\item \textbf{The external cohort is stage IIA to IIIC with no Normal-like case.} External CNV
0.888 against internal 0.866 therefore means ``does not degrade'', not ``improves'': different
case mixes under different protocols.

\item \textbf{Two internal CNV protocols differ by about 0.010, so name the one in use.} 0.872 is
the refit per CLAM fold (599 cases) used for every contrast here; 0.862 is
\texttt{StratifiedKFold(10, seed 0)} on the same cases, whose equal-weight mean is 0.922 rather
than 0.926; $0.866 \pm 0.003$ is the 5-fold by 10-reseed figure on the full 945-case fitting set
and is the right one for the CNV branch as a model in its own right. The spread exceeds several
contrasts called significant, which is why $\phi = 0.193$ (per-CLAM-fold) and $\phi = 0.269$
(StratifiedKFold) are always quoted with their protocol.

\item \textbf{Independent splits, no external operator evaluation.} CLAM's ten splits are drawn
independently rather than partitioned, so pooled WSI-only is a small ensemble flattered by roughly
$+0.01$ AUROC (footnote d of Table~\ref{tab:main}); and no trained operator can be evaluated
externally (footnote c), so every external fusion row is the untrained probability mean.

\item \textbf{Reproduction environment and determinism.} The console scripts are not installed on
\texttt{PATH} here, so the 2026-09-01 runs used
\texttt{python -m dpcode.cli.analysis <action>}; \texttt{pip install -e '.[dev]'} restores them, and
\texttt{dp-analysis} has \texttt{-{}-show-config} rather than \texttt{-{}-dry-run}. Nothing was
trained, downloaded or overwritten for this summary. Seeds, folds and split sets are fixed, but
run-to-run variance is unmeasured and bitwise reproducibility is not achievable in this stack.

\end{itemize}
